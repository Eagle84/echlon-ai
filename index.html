      How it works

      The request finds its own department

      You do not pick a team or a mode. A dispatch step reads the goal and hands it to the head who owns that work.

      A plan you approve

      Your goal becomes a task breakdown with an owner and acceptance criteria per task. Nothing is built until you say go.

      Work that is reviewed

      Every role names the reviewer who signs its work off. Results that fall short are sent back before you ever see them.

      Proof, not promises

      Lint and tests run as real commands, and a UI app must boot and serve. "It compiles" is not evidence.

      An unfinished plan does not ship

      Tasks are tracked from the plan to a recorded verdict. A plan still unfinished fails the run rather than being delivered half-done.

      Costs under control

      Per-phase and per-run spend caps, live cost by model, and one button that stops the whole company mid-task.
